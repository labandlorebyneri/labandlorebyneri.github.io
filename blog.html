The Three Cs

The framework behind the insights.

01

Character

Who we become. Identity, values, choices, growth, behavior, and the experiences that shape a person.

02

Creativity

How we respond. Ideas, experimentation, communication, problem solving, and alternatives when familiar answers stop working.

03

Community

How we move together. Groups, culture, institutions, organizations, collective behavior, and the systems we share.

How to read Lab & Lore

Lenses and series do different jobs.

Lens

The lens answers: what is this piece examining? Character, Creativity, Community, or sometimes more than one at once.

Series

The series answers: what kind of conversation is this? An inquiry in progress, a question of meaning, or an examination of what we hear, carry, and normalize together.

A series is not tied to a single C. The same series can move across different lenses as the question changes.

The framework

Character. Creativity. Community.

The Three Cs give the writing a way to look more closely without pretending every question has only one answer.
