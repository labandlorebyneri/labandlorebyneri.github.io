Writing by Neri C

Notes from the lab. Stories for the lore.

This is where I write through questions about people, behavior, creativity, work, technology, belief, organizations, society, and the systems we build around ourselves.

Some pieces are analytical. Some are personal. Some begin with research. Others begin with something I cannot stop thinking about.

Every piece sits somewhere within the same three lenses: Character, Creativity, and Community.
