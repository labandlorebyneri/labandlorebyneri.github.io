About Neri C

Professionally curious about why people do what they do.

I am Neri C, a creative behavioral scientist interested in the point where human behavior, creativity, research, technology, and real-world problems meet.

The path behind the questions

I have never been particularly interested in staying inside one lane.

I studied at the University of the Philippines Diliman, where research became more than an academic exercise for me. It became a way of approaching uncertainty.

Since then, my work has moved through research, digital marketing, strategy, project and program management, communication, public speaking, organizational questions, and community work. Different settings. Similar questions.

How do people think, decide, create, organize, and interact with the systems and people around them?

People make decisions inside systems. Groups develop norms. Organizations create incentives. Technology changes what is possible. Communication changes what people believe is possible. That is the territory I like exploring.

Four recurring territories

Different disciplines. Connected questions.

The labels change by context. The underlying inquiry is usually about people, systems, choices, and what happens when an idea has to work outside a document.

01

Research

Questions before conclusions. Curious enough to ask, structured enough to investigate, and flexible enough to change an answer when the evidence changes.

02

Strategy

Understanding before action. Good ideas are rarely enough. You have to understand people well enough to know which ideas deserve to become actions.

03

Projects & Programs

Where ideas meet reality. Resources, incentives, institutions, timing, behavior, and coordination determine whether an interesting idea becomes a workable one.

04

People & Community

Behavior is rarely a solo act. Behavior becomes clearer when people are participants in the conversation, not simply objects of observation.

My framework

Character. Creativity. Community.

The Three Cs are the core lenses I use to think about people, ideas, and collective life.

01

Character

Who we become. The individual, identity, values, choices, growth, and the experiences that shape a person.

02

Creativity

How we respond. Ideas, communication, experimentation, strategy, and alternatives when familiar answers stop working.

03

Community

How we move together. Groups, organizations, culture, institutions, collective behavior, and movements.

How I explore questions

Observe. Question. Test. Reflect.

Lab & Lore is not a catalogue of conclusions. It is a record of inquiry.

Observe

Notice behavior, language, incentives, contradictions, environments, systems, patterns, and details that are easy to dismiss.

Question

Challenge explanations that feel too convenient and assumptions that survive mostly because nobody has disturbed them lately.

Test

Compare alternatives through research, experimentation, implementation, conversation, evidence, and the useful inconvenience of being wrong.

Reflect

Ask what the result changes, what deserves to be remembered, and what new question appeared along the way.

Why Lab & Lore

The lab investigates. The lore remembers.

The lab represents inquiry. It is where I observe, question, experiment, research, challenge assumptions, change my mind, and occasionally discover that I was asking the wrong question in the first place.

The lore represents what survives that process. It is the lesson, contradiction, interpretation, story, experience, or realization worth carrying forward.

Character, Creativity, and Community give me three connected lenses for moving through those questions. The lab gives me a way to examine them. The lore gives me somewhere to preserve what I learn.

What is understood in the lab becomes lore for another generation to encounter, question, challenge, and build upon.
