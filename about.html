About Me

I am interested in how ideas take shape, how people make decisions, and how creativity, behavior, work, and everyday experience intersect.

What I am curious about

I am drawn to questions about people, systems, communication, technology, creativity, and the small details that influence how we think and behave.

Some of those questions become engagements. Others become notes, observations, experiments, or ideas that I want to think through in writing.

Why Lab & Lore exists

Lab & Lore is my personal space for both experimentation and reflection.

The lab is where ideas get tested, built, revised, and sometimes abandoned. The lore is what remains after experience, observation, and time.

This site is where I keep both.
