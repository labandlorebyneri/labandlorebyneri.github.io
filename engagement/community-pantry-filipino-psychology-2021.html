45th National Conference for Filipino Psychology

Presenting qualitative research on community pantries, Filipino values, collective responsibility, and civic response during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Role: Paper Presenter & Co-author Date: November 25 to 27, 2021 Format: National Conference
Paper Presented

Ang Community Pantry bilang Repleksyon ng Pagpapahalagang Pilipino at Panawagan ng Ordinaryong Mamamayan Para sa Pagbabago sa Gitna ng Pandemya: Isang Kwalitatibong Pananaliksik

The Research

Looking at community response through Filipino psychology.

I presented a qualitative research paper at the 45th National Conference for Filipino Psychology, organized by the National Association for Sikolohiyang Pilipino, Inc. in partnership with the Division of Social Sciences of UP Visayas Tacloban College.

The study examined the community pantry phenomenon during the COVID-19 pandemic as an expression of Filipino values and as a form of ordinary citizens' call for social change.

The research was conducted with Maria Theresa B. Largo and Adriel Jireh M. Lumague.

At a Glance
Method
Qualitative research
Subject
Community pantries
Context
COVID-19 pandemic
What I Contributed

Taking the research into a wider scholarly conversation.

I co-authored the qualitative study and served as the paper presenter at the conference, communicating the research, its findings, and its implications to participants.

Presenting the work beyond the classroom also meant placing our interpretation of community behavior in conversation with scholars and practitioners working within Sikolohiyang Pilipino.

What I Took From It

This experience showed me that everyday community behavior can become a meaningful subject of scientific inquiry. The community pantry was not only a response to material need. It also raised questions about kapwa, collective responsibility, civic participation, and how people respond when formal systems are under strain.
