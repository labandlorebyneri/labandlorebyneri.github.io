Engagement · Community Service

Legal & Medical Mission and Reproductive Health Screening

A multi-sector initiative bringing healthcare, legal assistance, local government, and community organizations together to expand access to services in Bataan.

Role: Collaborator Location: Bataan Date: October 2022
The Project

Bringing different kinds of help into one community effort.

I collaborated on a legal, medical, and reproductive health initiative that served more than 1,000 people from vulnerable sectors of society in Bataan.

The project brought together professionals, public institutions, healthcare providers, legal organizations, and local governments around a shared goal: making essential services more accessible to communities that could benefit from them.

Collaboration

One initiative, many institutions.

The mission involved collaboration across medical, legal, government, and community institutions.

  • Philippine Medical Association - Bataan Medical Society
  • Local Government Unit of Samal
  • Local Government Unit of Hermosa
  • Local Government Unit of Orani
  • Local Government Unit of Abucay
  • Public Attorney's Office
  • Integrated Bar of the Philippines - Bataan Chapter
  • Health and Human Development Foundation
  • Evangelista Medical Specialty Hospital
  • Department of Health
What I Contributed

Working across disciplines and institutions.

I contributed to an initiative that depended on coordination among organizations with different areas of expertise, responsibilities, and ways of working.

The experience placed community service within a wider network of healthcare professionals, legal practitioners, public agencies, local governments, and civil society organizations.

What I Took From It

The mission showed me what becomes possible when institutions with different expertise organize around a shared public need. Access problems rarely fit neatly within one profession or organization. Responding to them often requires coordination across healthcare, law, government, and community networks.
