Bangon Cagayan Donation Drive

A community-led relief initiative mobilizing support from Dinalupihan, Bataan for communities affected by Typhoon Ulysses in Cagayan Valley.

Role: Organizer Location: Dinalupihan, Bataan Date: November 2020
The Project

Turning concern into coordinated action.

In November 2020, I organized a donation drive with friends to support communities in Cagayan Valley affected by Typhoon Ulysses.

The initiative mobilized residents of Dinalupihan, Bataan and pooled approximately ₱150,000 worth of cash and goods for affected communities.

At a Glance
Mobilized
Approximately ₱150,000
Support
Cash and donated goods
Beneficiary Region
Cagayan Valley
What I Contributed

Organizing locally, connecting regionally.

I initiated and organized the donation drive, coordinated with friends and supporters, and used our networks to mobilize donations from residents of Dinalupihan.

I also reached out to UP Anna na Cagayan, a University of the Philippines Diliman student organization composed of students from the Cagayan Valley, to help connect the initiative with people who had closer knowledge of the region and its communities.

What I Took From It

The experience showed me that community action becomes more effective when concern is paired with local knowledge and trusted relationships. Mobilizing resources was only one part of the work. Knowing who could help connect those resources with the communities that needed them mattered just as much.
