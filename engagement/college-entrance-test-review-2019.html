College Entrance Test Review

A four-month review program designed to help senior high school students prepare more deliberately for college entrance examinations.

Role: Organizer Organization: Saint John Academy Date: May to August 2019
My first project, 2019.
The Project

Building a review program around preparation.

I organized a four-month intensive review program to help selected Saint John Academy senior high school students prepare for college entrance examinations.

Rather than treating preparation as a single review session, the program was structured across several months and covered the major areas students were expected to encounter in entrance examinations.

At a Glance
Duration
Four months
Audience
Senior high school students
Focus
College entrance examinations
What I Contributed

From idea to structured program.

I organized the review program and collaborated with professionals and practitioners in designing assessments for Language Proficiency, Science, Mathematics, and Reading Comprehension.

The project required bringing together different areas of expertise and translating them into a program students could actually use as part of their preparation.

What I Took From It

This was one of my earliest experiences building a structured program around a real need. It taught me that good preparation is not simply about providing more material. It is about identifying what people need to practice, designing the right structure around it, and bringing together people with the expertise to make that structure useful.
