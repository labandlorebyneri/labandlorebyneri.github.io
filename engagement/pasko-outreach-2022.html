Engagement · Community Outreach

Pasko para sa mga Bata & Paskong Katutubo

Two Christmas outreach initiatives organized for children in upland and Indigenous communities across Bataan.

Role: Organizer Location: Bataan Date: December 2022
The Project

Two communities, two forms of outreach.

In December 2022, I organized two Christmas outreach initiatives focused on children from communities in Bataan.

Although both projects were organized during the Christmas season, each was designed around a different community and form of support.

Initiative 01

Pasko para sa mga Bata

I organized a Christmas party for children living in nearby upland communities and distributed 200 packs containing school supplies, food, and clothing.

Initiative 02

Paskong Katutubo

I organized an outreach initiative that distributed coloring books and snacks to selected Indigenous children in the First District of Bataan, reaching approximately 1,500 children.

What I Contributed

Organizing outreach around the community being served.

I organized the two outreach activities and coordinated the preparation and distribution of the materials provided through each initiative.

The programs required different approaches. One centered on a Christmas gathering and practical supplies for children in upland communities. The other focused on distributing age-appropriate materials and snacks to Indigenous children across the First District of Bataan.

What I Took From It

These initiatives reinforced for me that community work is not only about scale. It is also about context. Different communities may call for different forms of support, and a useful response begins by paying attention to the people, circumstances, and needs involved.
