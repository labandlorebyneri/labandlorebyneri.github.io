Reflections

Leadership becomes visible when responsibility has consequences for other people.

Leadership is easy to describe as confidence, decisiveness, or influence. It becomes more complicated when other people are depending on the quality of your preparation, communication, judgment, and follow-through.

Development happens in that tension. Responsibility can reveal where confidence is useful, where certainty becomes dangerous, and where listening is not hesitation but part of making a better decision.

Through Character, the questions become personal: How is trust earned? What does authority require from the person holding it? When should a leader direct, and when should experience elsewhere in the room take precedence?
