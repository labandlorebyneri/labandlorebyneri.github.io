As a National Discipline Awardee, What Can I Do to Make This Country a Better Place to Live In?

An essay submitted as part of the AY Foundation National Discipline Award selection process, centered on discipline, responsibility, Christian values, education, family, and service.

Context

The prompt asked me to explain what discipline could contribute to the country.

I wrote this essay after being selected to submit an entry for the AY Foundation award. The prompt asked what I could do, as a National Discipline Awardee, to help make the country a better place to live in.

My answer was rooted in the world I knew at the time: school, church, family, academic work, student organizations, and youth service.

The essay is brief, but it captures how I understood discipline at that point in my life: as consistency between values, responsibilities, and action.

Discipline and character

The essay defines discipline through work, values, integrity, and responsibility.

The essay describes a National Discipline Awardee as someone who works hard, embodies Christian ideals and morals, performs duties as a citizen, and follows the standards of school and church without compromising personal values and integrity.

Education is presented as a springboard for improving oneself, one's family, and society.

How the essay develops the argument

Academic excellence is connected to preparation, gratitude, and service.

The paper presents school achievement and community involvement as part of the same discipline expected from a young person preparing for future responsibility.

01

Academic excellence

Academic performance is presented as a way of developing potential and preparing for future goals.

02

Balance and commitment

The essay describes the challenge of balancing schoolwork with organizations and religious service, particularly youth leadership in the parish.

03

Gratitude to family

Good grades are presented as a way of honoring parents whose work made education possible.

04

Service by example

The essay connects national service with carrying out responsibilities consistently and setting a good example for fellow students and young people in the community.

Conclusion

The future depends on what is done in the present.

The essay concludes that consistently fulfilling responsibilities to school, church, and community is already a contribution to society.

Present effort is framed as preparation for the future, while academic performance, service, and being a good example are presented as ways of serving the nation.
