Another Reason I Created This Space

On losing my mum, continuing the work she helped make possible, and understanding love through memory and faith.

By Neri September 13, 2026 Series: Love & Lord Lens: Character
Series
Love & Lord
Lens
Character
Published
Sep 13, 2026

Lab & Lore explained why I created this space: to examine questions, test ideas, document observations, and preserve what seems worth remembering.

But there is another reason.

Losing your mum at 26 changes the way you look at life, love, and even laughter.

So much of the work I have been able to do, the places I have gone, the people I have met, and the engagements I have taken part in would not have been possible without her. In ways both visible and quiet, she helped make that life possible.

Continuing the work now feels different.

The opportunities still come. The work continues. There are still places to go, people to meet, and things worth contributing to. But her presence is no longer something I can return to in the same way.

It is something I carry in memory, in the life she helped shape, and in the work I continue because she once made so much of it possible.

People often say that grief is another form of love. I think there is truth in that. But grief also made me pause.

It made me think about love as it existed here, in presence, conversation, sacrifice, laughter, and ordinary days.

And it made me think about love as it exists now through faith: beyond what I can see, and with the Lord.

That kind of love changes how you remember. It also changes how you continue.

Perhaps that is another reason this space exists.

To make room for life, love, laughter, loss, and the Lord.
