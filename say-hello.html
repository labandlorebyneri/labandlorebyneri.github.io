What Do We Talk About?

Start with the thing you cannot stop thinking about.

It could be a research question, a strange pattern you noticed, a behavioral problem inside an organization, an unconventional marketing idea, a disagreement with something I wrote, or a question about Character, Creativity, or Community.

It does not have to arrive fully formed. Some of the better conversations start before anyone knows exactly where they are going.

Bring a question. We can figure out what it wants to become.
