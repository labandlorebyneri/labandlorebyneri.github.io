What belongs here

Ideas that are still moving.

Lab & Lore is the most experimental of the three editorial series. It is where I can follow a question without pretending I already know where it ends.

The starting point may be psychology, technology, work, culture, communication, research, or an everyday observation. What matters is that the question remains open long enough to encounter evidence, context, contradiction, and another point of view.

Changing my mind is allowed here. So is discovering that the better question was not the one I started with.
