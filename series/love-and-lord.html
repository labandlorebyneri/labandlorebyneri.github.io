Series 02

Love & Lord

Questions of meaning.
What belongs here

The human questions that stay with us.

Some questions can be tested. Others are lived.

Love & Lord is where I write about the parts of human life that sit close to belief, responsibility, intimacy, conscience, grief, hope, and purpose. Psychology can tell us something about attachment. History can tell us how institutions and traditions change. Data can reveal patterns. But none of them can completely decide what a person ought to love, forgive, carry, or believe.

This series does not treat faith and reason as enemies. It gives both enough room to be taken seriously.

The lenses

Love & Lord can move through any of the Three Cs.

The series defines the conversation. Character, Creativity, and Community help reveal what each piece is really asking about.

01 · Character

What do we carry within us?

Identity, conscience, desire, faith, values, responsibility, love, doubt, and the choices that shape a person.

02 · Creativity

How do we imagine another way?

The stories, rituals, language, symbols, and acts of imagination through which people make meaning and respond to uncertainty.

03 · Community

What do we owe one another?

Belonging, family, faith communities, norms, care, exclusion, responsibility, and the moral life we build with other people.
