Loud & Load

What we hear. What we carry.

Life gets loud. Work gets heavy. Groups create expectations, organizations create pressures, and societies quietly decide which loads people are expected to carry.

Loud & Load is where I think through work, ambition, organizations, hierarchy, culture, pressure, mobility, collective expectations, and the demands people absorb from the systems around them.

The loud is what surrounds us. The load is what stays with us. This series asks how one becomes the other.

What Belongs Here

The weight of collective life.

This series looks at the pressures created when individual behavior meets organizations, institutions, economic systems, social expectations, hierarchy, and collective ambition.

Sometimes that means examining work. Sometimes it means thinking about leadership, class, status, mobility, burnout, organizational behavior, or why groups normalize demands that individuals would never create on their own.
